Free · Plays in your browser · 2–8 players

Buy, trade and build your way to the top.

Trade Tycoon is a Monopoly-style property-trading game. Pass the device around the table, or open a room and play online with friends — no sign-up, no install.

Play now How to play

Everything you expect from a night of property trading

  • Two ways to play

    Hotseat on one shared device, or online in a room: share a code and everyone joins from their own screen.

  • Real trading

    Offer cash, properties and Get Out of Jail Free cards. The other player reviews and accepts or declines.

  • Auctions

    Pass on a property and it goes to auction, so nothing sits unowned while the rest of the table bids.

  • Build and mortgage

    Complete a colour set, build houses and hotels evenly, and mortgage to raise cash when rent comes due.

  • Turn timers online

    A three-minute turn clock and a 30-second bid clock keep the game moving when someone wanders off. The server enforces them, so every screen agrees.

  • Accessible by design

    Every player has a distinct colour and shape, and board tiles are labelled for screen readers. It adapts to phones, tablets and wide screens.

How to play

  1. Pick a mode

    Choose Local Multiplayer to pass one device around, or Online Multiplayer to create a room and share its code with friends.

  2. Roll and move

    Everyone starts with $1,500. Roll two dice, move your piece, and collect $200 each time you pass Go. Doubles roll again, but three in a row sends you to jail.

  3. Buy, rent and trade

    Land on an unowned property to buy it or send it to auction. Landing on someone else’s costs rent. Trade with other players to complete colour sets.

  4. Outlast everyone

    Build houses and hotels to raise the rent. When you can’t pay a debt, declare bankruptcy. The last player standing wins.

Under the hood

An open-source TypeScript monorepo. All the game rules live in one pure package that the app and the server share, so they can never disagree about what is legal.

  • Client

    Expo (React Native) with React 19, rendering to the web, iOS and Android from one codebase.

  • Server

    Node.js and Express. Plain REST for actions and Server-Sent Events for live updates — no WebSockets.

  • Game logic

    A framework-free, immutable reducer written in TypeScript and covered by Vitest tests.

  • Hosting

    Vercel for the app and API, Upstash Redis for room state, and a standby copy on Render.
